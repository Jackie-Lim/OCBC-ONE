# OCBC ONE: 2-Minute Demo Script

Before presenting, open **Demo scenarios** and select **Reset Tom's journey**. Keep the optional consent choices enabled.

## 0:00–0:15: The promise

**Say:** “OCBC ONE turns disconnected financial products into one living plan. Tom chooses what data it may understand, separately from what it may do.”

**Show:** Save the consent choices, then open **Demo scenarios**.

## 0:15–0:40: Tier 1: first salary

**Click:** **First full-time salary**, then **View calculation**.

**Say:** “Tom's first salary arrives. His saved rule protects S$3,000 and caps transfers at S$1,000, so ONE moves exactly S$1,000 to emergency savings. It is bounded, explained and reversible.”

**Click:** **View AI state**.

**Say:** “Behind the plan is a real deep-clustering demo. An autoencoder compresses six consented financial features, and K-means recognises Tom as **Buffer Building**.”

## 0:40–1:08: Tier 2: Dubai trip

**Click:** Close the state engine, run **Trip to Dubai**, then choose **View AI state** from the home card.

**Say:** “A dated booking and connected policy data change his fingerprint. The model now detects **Protection Gap**, but that label cannot buy anything.”

**Click:** **Compare cover**, select a plan, review it and confirm.

**Say:** “ONE prepares the comparison; Tom still chooses the provider, cover and price.”

## 1:08–1:42: Tier 3 and investments

**Click:** Run **Planning a first home**, open **Investments**, then choose **View AI state**.

**Say:** “With more than three months of emergency savings and recurring surplus, the state moves to **Growth Ready**. ONE can open an investment suitability review and let Tom test a monthly amount, but it cannot select a product or place an order.”

**Click:** Close the state engine and briefly move the investment slider.

**Say:** “For the mortgage, ONE stops completely. It prepares the facts and hands the decision to an OCBC specialist.”

## 1:42–2:00: Close

**Click:** Open **Trust controls** or **ONE decisions**.

**Say:** “That is the Trust Ladder: ONE acts only inside reversible rules, Tom confirms product decisions, and a human takes over for high-impact choices. The AI identifies what changed; rules calculate what should change; the customer controls what may happen.”

## If time is tight

Show only the AI state after the Dubai event and say:

> “Six consented features become a two-dimensional financial fingerprint. Deep clustering detects a Protection Gap, but deterministic rules, customer permission and the Trust Ladder, not the model, control every action.”
